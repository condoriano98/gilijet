# QA run: the booking flow and the customer pages on a 375 px phone

**Date:** 2026-10-31. **Target:** local only (Docker Postgres, `seed:qa` plus local ports and legs, outbound keys blanked, the three local WhatsApp variables set as in `README.md` here). **Workers:** 2. **Uncommitted** at the time of the run.

## What was added

- Playwright project **`mobile-flow`** (Pixel 5 device settings, viewport 375 x 667). It runs the booking, round-trip, lookup, search, form, detail and home-search specs a second time on a phone, after the `chromium` project has finished (they share fixtures, so they cannot run together; if `chromium` fails, `mobile-flow` is skipped).
- `mobile-sweep.spec.ts`: 15 pages that need no data. MOB-001 no sideways scroll, MOB-002 nothing covers an enabled submit button, MOB-003 the consent banner leaves room, MOB-004 submit buttons are at least 44 px high (runs only for routes in `REDESIGNED_ROUTES`, empty today).
- BOOK-M01 (booking form), BOOK-M02 (booking page, three statuses), BOOK-M03 (results page): no sideways scroll, submit buttons reachable.
- Helpers in `tests/e2e/support/overflow.ts`: `expectSubmitButtonsReachable`, `expectSubmitButtonsBigEnough`.

## What it found

| Class | Finding |
|---|---|
| **App bug, new (B12)** | The booking page `/b/<reference>` is **391 px wide on a 375 px phone**, so it scrolls sideways (widest element `div.flex-1.space-y-2`). Not visible at desktop width. |
| **Design standard, not a regression (B13)** | Buttons below the 44 px floor of the new design: "Look up" on `/b` 40 px, "Cancel booking" 36 px, the "Book 1 · ..." link on search 40 px. MOB-004 guards a route once it is listed in `REDESIGNED_ROUTES`. |
| Spec wrong, fixed | A "covered button" alarm on the home page: the Search button was disabled (`pointer-events: none`) and the point test named the form behind it. The helper now skips disabled buttons. |
| Good news | The whole booking flow (search, form validation, booking, pay redirects, detail, cancel, reschedule guards, lookup) passes on a 375 px phone. The 15 data-free pages do not scroll sideways, and no enabled submit button is covered. |
